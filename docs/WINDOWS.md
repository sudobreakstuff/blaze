# Giving Blaze to Jasmine on Windows

Blaze is just a website, so **there is nothing to install for it to work** — she
opens the link and he's there:

> **https://sudobreakstuff.github.io/blaze/**

But Windows can make it feel like a real little app. Here's the nicest way.

---

## 1. Open it (the only required step)

Double-click the link, or paste it into the address bar. Best in **Microsoft
Edge** (Windows' built-in browser) or **Google Chrome**.

> Use Edge if you can — it ships Microsoft's *natural* voices, so Blaze will
> sound genuinely human. Chrome is good too.

---

## 2. Install it as an app (recommended)

This gives Blaze his own window, a desktop shortcut and a Start-menu entry — no
browser tabs.

**Microsoft Edge**
1. Open the link.
2. Click the **⋯** menu (top-right) → **Apps** → **Install this site as an app**.
3. Name it "Blaze" → **Install**.
4. It opens in its own window. Right-click the taskbar icon → **Pin to taskbar**.

**Google Chrome**
1. Open the link.
2. Click the **⋮** menu (top-right) → **Cast, save and share** →
   **Install page as app** (sometimes it's **Save and share → Install**).
3. Name it "Blaze" → **Install**.

You can also just look for a small **install icon (⊕ / monitor)** at the right
end of the address bar and click it.

---

## 3. First-run permissions (optional)

- **Notifications** — when Blaze asks, click **Allow**, so he can gently nudge
  her. If it's blocked, the ⚙ settings toggle will explain.
- **Microphone** — only if she wants to *talk* to Blaze; click **Allow**.

---

## 4. His voice

On Windows, Edge/Chrome expose Microsoft's voices (for example *Microsoft Guy*,
*David*, *Andrew*, or Edge's *… Online (Natural)* ones). Blaze automatically
picks the most natural **male** voice.

To change it: ⚙ (settings) → **Voice pick**, choose a **♂** voice, press **▶** to
hear it, and nudge **Pitch** (~0.85) and **Speed** (~0.95) to taste.

---

## 5. Photos

In ⚙ → **Wall photos → ＋ add**, or just **drag pictures onto the room**. They
pin onto Blaze's wall.

> Her photos are saved **only on her PC** (in the browser's local storage). They
> are never uploaded, and nobody else can see them. The rest of the site is a
> public page.

---

## 6. Handy extras (optional)

- **Pin to taskbar / Start:** right-click Blaze's icon → *Pin to taskbar*.
- **Start with Windows:** press `Win+R`, type `shell:startup`, and drop a
  shortcut to the installed Blaze app in that folder.
- **Offline:** after the first load it keeps working without internet.
- **Meet him:** Blaze greets her by name-ish, remembers things she tells him,
  sends her places on the internet, makes her little gifts, tells terrible
  jokes and stories with morals. Tapping him pets him.

---

## If something looks off

- **No sound?** Check Windows volume / the site isn't muted in the browser tab,
  and that the ⚙ **Voice** toggle is on. Test with **▶** in settings.
- **He sounds robotic?** She may be in a browser without the Microsoft natural
  voices — Edge is the fix. (On Linux you'd install one; see `VOICE.md`.)
- **Want to start over?** ⚙ → **forget** clears what he remembers; the photo
  gallery has per-photo delete (×).
